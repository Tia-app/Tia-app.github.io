About TIA

A messenger that is more than a chat.

What is TIA?

TIA is a Persian-first messenger for Android and Windows. It has chats, groups and stories, plus an AI assistant built into the app.

More Than a Chat

The slogan reflects our idea that a messenger is more than a place to send messages. TIA aims to make repetitive chat tasks, like reading long messages, translating and writing replies, easier.

Platforms

Android (via Myket) and Windows 10 and 11.

Creator

TIA is built by Taha. The source of this website is public on GitHub .
